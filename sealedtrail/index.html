The problem

paper_trail tracks changes. Auditors ask about access.

Change-history gems are great for "what did this record look like yesterday?" But when an audit comes, the questions are different, and most Rails teams end up hand-rolling the answers the week before the auditor arrives.

👁️

"Who viewed this patient's record?"

Change-tracking gems only log writes. Logging reads and exports of sensitive data means custom controller hooks in every action.

🔏

"How do you know nobody edited the log?"

An audits table any admin can UPDATE isn't evidence. You need to prove integrity.

🗄️

"What's your retention policy, and is it enforced?"

Versions tables quietly grow to hundreds of GB, while GDPR erasure requests collide with "never delete audit data".

Frequently asked questions

When does it ship?+

Founding customers get the first private beta in December 2026, with new builds as features land, and v1.0 by 31 January 2027. If v1.0 isn't delivered by then, you get a full refund on request.

Do I have to remove paper_trail or audited?+

No. Keep using them for versioning and undo if you like. SealedTrail adds access logging, integrity and retention on top, and can import your existing history.

Does my data leave my servers?+

No. SealedTrail is a gem, not a SaaS. Logs live in your own database (and optionally your own S3 bucket). That makes it simpler for PHI and personal data, because there's no extra sub-processor or BAA.

Won't logging every read slow my app down?+

Read logging is opt-in per model and written in batches after the response, with an async ActiveJob mode for high-traffic endpoints.

How does "tamper-evident" work with GDPR erasure?+

Each entry's hash covers a fingerprint of its personal data, not the raw values. Erasure redacts the values but keeps the fingerprint, so the chain still verifies.

Does this make us SOC 2 / HIPAA compliant?+

No tool can do that by itself. SealedTrail covers the audit-logging part and produces evidence your auditor can review. Policies, access reviews and the rest of your programme are still yours.

What does the licence cover?+

One company and unlimited apps, delivered through a private gem source. Renew yearly for updates and support. If you stop renewing, the version you have keeps working.
